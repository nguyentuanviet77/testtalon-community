[Documentation home](../README.md) | See also: [Community and Commercial editions](editions.md)

## Contact

Email **support@testtalon.com** for questions about your license, installation, documented configuration, documentation feedback and bug reports.

To report a vulnerability, email support@testtalon.com privately. Do not describe it in a public issue.

## What support covers

Commercial support covers installation, documented configuration and the included platform features. It does not automatically cover:

- custom framework development
- customer-specific test implementation
- infrastructure administration
- debugging your application
- custom CI/CD engineering
- database administration
- security consulting
- custom third-party integration

Customer-developed changes, extensions and third-party integrations are not covered unless agreed. Additional professional services may be offered separately. Third-party components keep their own licenses, and you are responsible for the accounts, subscriptions and infrastructure that they need.
